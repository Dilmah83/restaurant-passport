Restaurant Passport privacy

The native app keeps visits, saved places, ratings, spending, photos and notes on your device. Device backups follow your Apple settings. Full JSON exports include photos and notes; share them only with intended recipients. There is no account or automatic cloud sync.

Apple Maps receives the area you choose when searching for nearby restaurants. Location permission is optional and requested only when you tap Use my location. You can enter an area instead. Optional Google popularity lookup sends a coordinate rounded to three decimal places and a selected radius through this server to Google Places. Visit history, personal ratings and photos are not included. Popularity results are transient and not cached in trip backups. A Google place ID may be retained with your own name and notes.

The discovery access code is stored in the iPhone Keychain, outside exports. The Google API key stays on the server. Hosting providers and Google may process request metadata such as IP addresses and timestamps. The server applies short-lived in-memory rate limits. See the Google Privacy Policy and Apple's privacy information for their services.

Delete local records in the app, and remove separate exports or backups independently. Uninstalling removes local app data subject to backups; Keychain items can persist. Stock background food photos are bundled and do not trigger remote image requests.
